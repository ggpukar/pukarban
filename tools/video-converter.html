Video and audio converter

Convert video and audio between common formats. Your files stay on your device.

Before you start

  • Formats: save as MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, AVI, FLV, GIF, MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or FLAC. Most common video and audio files can be opened.
  • Speed: your own device does the work, so a long video can take much longer than its running time. A computer works best.
  • First use: the converter downloads about 32 MB once, so use Wi-Fi if you can.

Built with ffmpeg.wasm and FFmpeg, which is GPL-licensed. Source is available at those links.
