QR code generator

Create a QR code for a link, any text or a photo.

Good to know

  • Photos are deleted automatically after 7 days, and then the QR code stops working.
  • Your photo is resized before upload, and hidden details such as where it was taken are removed.
  • Don't share private photos. Anyone who scans the code or gets the link can see it.
  • You can create up to 10 QR codes an hour.

Always test a QR code with your phone before printing it. Shared photos are stored with Cloudflare and deleted after 7 days.
