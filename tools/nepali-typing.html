  • Capital letters change the sound, so type in small letters unless the table shows a capital.
  • Put _ after a letter to join it by hand or end on a half letter: g_yaas gives ग्यास.
  • A full stop gives ।. Type two full stops for a normal one.
  • Put English words in curly brackets to keep them: {Facebook}.

This follows spelling rules, not a dictionary, so read your text through. It works on your device and nothing is sent.
