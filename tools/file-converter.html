File converter

Convert documents, spreadsheets, slides, PDFs and images. Your files stay on your device.

What can it convert?

  • Documents: PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, TSV, TXT, MD, HTML, JSON and XML files can be opened. They can be saved as PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, TXT, HTML, PNG or JPG.
  • Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG, AVIF and ICO files can be saved as PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, PDF, DOCX or PPTX.
  • Good to know: this runs in your browser, so it keeps your text and tables rather than the exact design. For a perfect copy of a designed Word or PowerPoint file, export to PDF from Word or PowerPoint.
  • Not supported: old .doc and .ppt files, password-protected files, and reading text from scanned PDFs or photos.
